# Learner: start here

Welcome to the SozoRock AI Capability Curriculum.

You are not being asked to memorize a product. You are building a repeatable way to frame, test, secure, govern, and explain AI-enabled work.

## Before the first session

1. Read [policies/lab-safety.md](../policies/lab-safety.md).
2. Read [BYOD_SETUP.md](BYOD_SETUP.md).
3. Choose a low-risk work or community problem.
4. Do not bring confidential, personal, protected health, or client data.
5. Create a folder for your evidence portfolio.

## How each mission works

Understand the problem. Observe the baseline. Build a bounded version. Break it safely. Improve it. Document what happened. Explain the remaining uncertainty.

## Your portfolio

Keep:

- version and date of the tool or model used
- inputs and outputs
- test cases and results
- decisions and assumptions
- safety checks
- human-review boundaries
- final reflection

Use [SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md](SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md) for naming and submission rules.
