# Code of Conduct

SozoRock AI Capability Curriculum is a learning and collaboration project. Participants are expected to:

- Treat learners, trainers, contributors, and affected communities with respect.
- Use plain, constructive, and evidence-based communication.
- Welcome questions and different levels of technical experience.
- Give credit to contributors and source material.
- Protect personal, confidential, and sensitive information.
- Report safety, accessibility, or conduct concerns through a private channel when possible.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, unauthorized system testing, publishing secrets, and deliberate misrepresentation of evidence.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions when necessary to protect learners, contributors, or the public.
