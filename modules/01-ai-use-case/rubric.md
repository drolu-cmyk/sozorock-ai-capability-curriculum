# Module 01 Rubric

## Scoring

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Workflow and problem clarity | 20 |
| User, stakeholders, and human accountability | 15 |
| Data identification and safety boundary | 20 |
| Non-AI baseline and success measures | 20 |
| Risk identification and initial controls | 15 |
| Test cases and reflection | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Pass rule

- 70 points or higher
- No critical safety violation
- All required deliverables submitted

## Critical safety violations

A critical safety violation includes:

- Uploading protected or confidential information without authorization
- Exposing a credential or API key
- Attempting an unauthorized system action
- Designing an unreviewed irreversible decision
- Misrepresenting an AI output as verified evidence

A critical safety violation requires remediation before the learner continues.
