# M11 learner mission: Capstone Build

## Mission

Practice how to integrate the curriculum into one bounded AI system. Practice how to demonstrate measurable usefulness and safe failure. Practice how to connect security, governance, and GRC evidence. Practice how to communicate tradeoffs to a decision-maker.

## Time and prerequisites

- Time: 240 minutes
- Prerequisite: M10
- Work in the approved lab workspace.
- Use public, synthetic, or approved de-identified data only.

## Safety first

Do not test an unauthorized system, scan, exploit, exfiltrate, or disrupt anything external. Do not place secrets, credentials, personal information, protected health information, or confidential client material in the lab or portfolio. Stop before any irreversible action and ask for human approval.

## Steps

1. Select a low-risk problem with a clear user and a human decision boundary.
2. Define the system purpose, non-goals, data sources, permissions, workflow, and success measures.
3. Build a working prototype or a fully specified manual/no-code equivalent in the approved lab.
4. Add an evaluation set with at least ten cases and record quality, safety, and failure results.
5. Add a threat model, permission map, approval gate, incident playbook, AI inventory, risk register, and control matrix.
6. Run a change or failure test and show how the system detects, contains, or escalates it.
7. Prepare a five-minute user demonstration and a one-page decision brief.
8. Submit the working artifact or reproducible specification and the complete evidence portfolio.

## Required submission

Submit:

- working-system and evidence-portfolio
- the test cases and observed results
- the tool/model/version and date, if a tool was used
- the data and source boundary
- the limitation or residual risk
- the human-review or approval boundary

## Pathways

- Core: complete the task with the repository templates and a manual fallback.
- Build: use an approved low-code, API, or code implementation.
- Stretch: add a stronger evaluation, automation, or governance improvement without widening the safety boundary.

## Success check

You are finished when another person can understand what you built, reproduce the stated test, see where a human must decide, and identify what remains uncertain.
