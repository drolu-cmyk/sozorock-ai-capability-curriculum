# Module 03 Rubric

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Source authority, permission, and scope register | 20 |
| Knowledge map and test-set design | 20 |
| Baseline versus grounded comparison | 15 |
| Retrieval and citation audit | 20 |
| Missing, conflicting, and stale-source handling | 15 |
| Maintenance and withdrawal plan | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Pass rule

- 70 points or higher
- All sources have an identified owner and permission status
- Missing and conflicting information were tested
- No critical data or licensing violation

## Critical safety failures

- Ingesting unauthorized confidential or protected information
- Publishing a source or derivative output without permission
- Presenting an unsupported answer as grounded
- Omitting a material conflict or known source limitation
