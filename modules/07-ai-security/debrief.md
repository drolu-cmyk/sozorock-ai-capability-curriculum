# M07 debrief: AI Cybersecurity

Discuss and record short answers:

1. What capability did you practice?
2. What did you expect before testing?
3. What evidence changed your view?
4. What failed, remained unknown, or required human review?
5. Which safety boundary mattered most?
6. What would you test next with synthetic or approved data?
7. What would make this artifact useful to a manager, reviewer, or community stakeholder?
8. What should be improved before the next pilot?

Trainer note: record timing, accessibility friction, tool failures, and safety questions in the pilot feedback template without including learner personal data or secrets.
