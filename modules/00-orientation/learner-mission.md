# M00 learner mission: Orientation and Safe Lab Practice

## Mission

Practice how to explain the program safety boundary. Practice how to choose a low-risk AI capability problem. Practice how to create a baseline and evidence folder. Practice how to describe a human-review boundary.

## Time and prerequisites

- Time: 90 minutes
- Prerequisite: none
- Work in the approved lab workspace.
- Use public, synthetic, or approved de-identified data only.

## Safety first

Do not test an unauthorized system, scan, exploit, exfiltrate, or disrupt anything external. Do not place secrets, credentials, personal information, protected health information, or confidential client material in the lab or portfolio. Stop before any irreversible action and ask for human approval.

## Steps

1. Read the safety policy and write three activities that are outside the lab boundary.
2. Complete the baseline: describe your current AI experience, one task you can perform without AI, and one uncertainty you want to investigate.
3. Choose a low-risk work or community problem. Do not use a real client record, health record, account, or private person.
4. Write the user, desired outcome, non-goals, success measure, and human-review boundary.
5. Run the task manually or with an approved AI tool and save the result as a baseline.
6. Create the portfolio folders and record the tool, version, date, data boundary, and limitations.
7. Submit the learner agreement, baseline, use-case note, and one question for the trainer.

## Required submission

Submit:

- learner-agreement, baseline, and evidence-folder
- the test cases and observed results
- the tool/model/version and date, if a tool was used
- the data and source boundary
- the limitation or residual risk
- the human-review or approval boundary

## Pathways

- Core: complete the task with the repository templates and a manual fallback.
- Build: use an approved low-code, API, or code implementation.
- Stretch: add a stronger evaluation, automation, or governance improvement without widening the safety boundary.

## Success check

You are finished when another person can understand what you built, reproduce the stated test, see where a human must decide, and identify what remains uncertain.
