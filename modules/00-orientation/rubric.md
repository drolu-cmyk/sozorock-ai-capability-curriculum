# M00 rubric: Orientation and Safe Lab Practice

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Safety boundary and data handling | 30 |
| Use-case clarity and human boundary | 25 |
| Baseline evidence | 20 |
| Portfolio hygiene | 15 |
| Reflection and next question | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

## Pass rule

Pass at 70 or higher with no critical safety failure. A learner may resubmit evidence after feedback.

## Critical safety failures

- Uses secrets, personal information, protected health information, or confidential client data.
- Tests or accesses an unauthorized system or account.
- Performs or enables an irreversible external action without approval.
- Hides a material failure or claims evidence that was not collected.
