# Accessibility and inclusion

The curriculum should be usable by people with different devices, bandwidth, languages, learning preferences, and levels of technical experience.

## Delivery requirements

- Use heading structure and plain language.
- Provide instructions in text before or alongside a demonstration.
- Do not make color the only signal.
- Allow typed, spoken, visual, or diagram-based explanations when the evidence standard is preserved.
- Provide a low-bandwidth and no-code path.
- Allow additional processing time.
- Provide downloadable copies of essential instructions.
- Keep examples culturally and professionally varied.
- Avoid requiring personal disclosure to demonstrate learning.

## Trainer check

Before a session, ask whether learners need:

- larger text or contrast support
- captions or transcripts
- keyboard-only operation
- extra time
- alternative file formats
- a manual or low-bandwidth workflow
- private submission

Accommodations should not require the learner to publish personal information in the public repository.
