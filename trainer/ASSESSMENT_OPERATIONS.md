# Trainer assessment operations

## Assessment principles

Assess observable work. Do not grade a learner on access to a paid tool, speed of typing, accent, or ability to reveal hidden model reasoning.

## Before delivery

- Confirm the rubric and pass threshold.
- Prepare an equivalent non-code path.
- Confirm how evidence will be anonymized.
- Decide how resubmissions work.
- Prepare feedback language for safe failure and uncertainty.

## During review

Check:

- problem and user boundary
- evidence quality
- test design
- safety and privacy
- human oversight
- explanation of limitations
- quality of the improvement

## Feedback format

1. Evidence observed
2. Capability demonstrated
3. Risk or gap
4. One specific next action
5. Whether resubmission is required

## Completion

A learner completes a module when the rubric threshold is met and critical safety failures are absent. A completion statement should identify the modules completed; it should not imply certification, licensure, audit assurance, or legal compliance.
