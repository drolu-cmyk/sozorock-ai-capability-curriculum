# Safe lab setup

## Approved lab boundary

Use public information, synthetic data, or data explicitly approved for the exercise. Work in a sandbox or approved workspace.

## Prohibited activity

Do not:

- scan or exploit external systems;
- bypass access controls;
- exfiltrate data;
- test someone else's account;
- upload secrets or sensitive data;
- trigger payments, messages, account changes, or other irreversible actions;
- use generated content to impersonate a real person.

## Recommended controls

- disable external actions by default;
- use least-privilege accounts;
- require human approval before side effects;
- keep a small test set;
- record model and tool versions;
- save evidence without secrets;
- delete temporary data after the session.

## If something goes wrong

Stop. Preserve only safe evidence. Do not copy or redistribute the sensitive value. Notify the trainer and repository owner through a private channel.
