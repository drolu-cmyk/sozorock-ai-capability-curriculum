# AI Use-Case Canvas

## 1. Workflow

What work is being improved?

## 2. User

Who uses the system?

## 3. Affected people

Who could be affected by the output?

## 4. Current process

How is the work done today?

## 5. Proposed AI task

What exact task will AI perform?

## 6. Inputs and outputs

What enters the system? What does it produce?

## 7. Data classification

Identify public, internal, confidential, personal, and highly sensitive data.

## 8. Non-AI baseline

What is the simplest alternative?

## 9. Success measures

How will quality, speed, cost, safety, and human workload be measured?

## 10. Risks and controls

What could go wrong, and what control will reduce each risk?

## 11. Human oversight

Who reviews, corrects, approves, appeals, or stops the system?

## 12. Recommendation

Proceed, proceed with conditions, redesign, or do not proceed.
