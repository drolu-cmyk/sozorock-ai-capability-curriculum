# AI incident playbook

## Trigger

Describe what happened and how it was detected.

## Immediate containment

- Stop or disable the affected workflow if needed.
- Preserve safe evidence.
- Prevent further data disclosure or external side effects.
- Notify the accountable owner.

## Triage

- What system, model, tool, and version were involved?
- What data was present?
- Who or what could be affected?
- Is the issue reproducible with synthetic data?
- Is a legal, privacy, safety, or security escalation required?

## Recovery

- Correct or withdraw the affected output.
- Restore a known-good version or manual fallback.
- Notify impacted people through the approved process.
- Record residual risk.
